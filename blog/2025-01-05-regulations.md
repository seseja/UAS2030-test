Dear Faculty & IRC members,  


Below are the main drone regulations and recommended standards if you are plaining to work with a drone. These guidelines should prevent potential issues in the future.  

## Regulations:  
•	The overall wight of the drone should not exceed 4 kg (This includes everything such as the batteries & payload). If your project requires more than that then you should provide a justification and permission from the department.  
o	Please note that this is a university not a company. We should focus on the concept/prototype and not the final complete product.  
o	Large drones are dangerous to use.  
o	It is unfortunate that in the last years some students have worked on large drones without even flying them once. This is an unacceptable waste of the university’s financial resources.  


•	The propeller should always be detached from the drone. The propellers should not be installed except if you are about to fly the drone.  


•	The lipo batteries should also not be used except for testing or before flying the drone.  


•	Whomever is working with the lipo batteries should be aware of its hazards.  


•	Students are prohibited to fly the drone in KFUPM campus. This should only be done by the department.  


## Recommended Standards:  
•	Drone frames:  
o	X500  
o	F330  
o	F450  
o	F550  
o	If you do not have one of these drones, then we generally recommend using a small frame. The size of the drone from should not exceed 500 mm  


•	Flight Controllers :  
o	Pixhawk 6C  
o	Pixhawk 6X  
o	Pixhawk 2.4.8 (this is generally not recommended but we have included it because it is cheaper and very common. If you are going to use it make sure to git the kit and keep in mind that the wires are not compatible with the rest of the flight controllers.  
o	Pixhawk orange cube  
o	CUAV V5 nano  
o	CUAV V5+  


•	Wires/cable/connection:  
o	JST GH 1.25. this type of connection is compatible with all the flight controllers except for Pixhawk 2.4.8.  
o	XT60 is the recommended lipo batter connection.  
o	Bullet size for ESCs are usually 3.5 mm (inner diameter).  
o	The wire AWG should be about 12 and 14 (used with batteries, ESCs, and motors). The rest of the parts generally requires less AWG. 


•	Propellers:  
o	The recommended size is about 10 inch in diameter and 4.5-inch pitch for most applications.  
o	It is recommended to use plastic propellers instead of carbon fiber (for students’ safety).  
•	Screws: It has been noticed that the M3*8 & M2.5*6 screws are the most common type of screws needed to work on a drone project especially if you will work on the F450 frame (M3*8 is used to mount the motor & M2.5*6 for the frame itself).  
•	The FS-i6X remote and the FS-iA6B or FS-iA10B receivers
