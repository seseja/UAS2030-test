### 2.1.3-Books:


-ROS Robot Programing --- By YoonSeok Pyo and others #this is the main book for our COE 510 course
in 2020  
-Robot Operating System for Absolute Beginners --- By Lentin Joseph #This book is one of the easiest
books and it is convenient compared to other resources. what is special about it is that unlike the rest of the
recourses it gives you the basics such as Ubuntu installation and terminal commands, C++, and python.  
-A Gentle Introduction to ROS --- By Jason M. O’Kane #I have read this book and I recommend it
because it is relatively easy and short. there are some topics which might not be covered in detail in other
resources such as Graph resource names and launch files and namespaces
