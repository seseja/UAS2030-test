2-Resources

All the resources will be sorted from the most to least important. please note that this is according to my opinion, and you may have different priorities. some resources I am not be very familiar with (but they seemed good) therefore I have placed them in the list (but not very high). Also note that these resources are not only useful for ROS but for the whole Robotics program

2.1-ROS Resources:​

2.1.1-Dr.Aniz Koubaa:​

The best place to learn ROS in my opinion is from Dr.Aniz Koubaa Udemy courses. He has other very useful courses such as self-driving cars. here are his courses related to ROS:
https://www.udemy.com/course/ros-essentials/
https://www.udemy.com/course/ros-navigation/
https://www.udemy.com/course/ros-web-nav/ #we did not take this one in COE 510

2.1.3-Books:​

-ROS Robot Programing --- By YoonSeok Pyo and others #this is the main book for our COE 510 course in 2020
-Robot Operating System for Absolute Beginners --- By Lentin Joseph #This book is one of the easiest books and it is convenient compared to other resources. what is special about it is that unlike the rest of the recourses it gives you the basics such as Ubuntu installation and terminal commands, C++, and python.
-A Gentle Introduction to ROS --- By Jason M. O’Kane #I have read this book and I recommend it because it is relatively easy and short. there are some topics which might not be covered in detail in other resources such as Graph resource names and launch files and namespaces
